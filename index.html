Card-Hopper-Smith

Card-Hopper-Smith is a jazz oriented trio playing together for over 20 years. Focused on mostly jazz standards in an improvisational format with many styles including swing, latin, bossanova, funk, and contemporary.

The smaller format allows for the emphasis on closer listening and interactivity between the players and the ability to stretch out in arrangements and solos. We are interested in live venues that welcome jazz instrumental music.
